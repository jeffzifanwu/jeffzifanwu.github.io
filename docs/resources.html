5. Production theory

[9:20] Marginal production of labour

  • “The diminishing marginal product of labour arises from the notion of short-run production constraints, where capital remains fixed.”

[18:13] Marginal rate of technical substitution; [25:03] Return to scale; [31:39] Productivity.

[9:13] Cost function, marginal cost of production, [41:00] Long-run cost curve (expansion path)

  • [13:40] The marginal cost of production is related to the marginal production of labour through the wage rate.
  • [32:55] The cost function is derived from a production function, labour costs, and capital costs.
  • [44:50] Both the long-run and short-run cost curves are u-shape (see explanations and examples in Varian and Melitz (2024), chapter 22)
